Info — packs, coins & series

Each pack has 6 cards, shown from worst to best. Good cards are rare, so some packs have none. Rarity depends on the estimated popularity rank (table above).

Pack shop: the free pack uses the normal odds. The Rare pack (100 coins) only draws characters ranked 10,000 or better and guarantees at least 1 Rare. The Epic pack (500 coins) only draws characters ranked 2,500 or better and guarantees at least 1 Epic.

Duplicates earn coins: 5 coins base, multiplied by the card's rarity tier (Common ×1 … Legendary ×5).

Series collections: own 4 different characters from the same anime to complete its collection. Each completed series grants a guaranteed Legendary in your next pack.
